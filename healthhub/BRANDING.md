# Health Hub image

The Health Hub image represents the main hub in repository documentation, login-page branding, and other large interface placements. It is distinct from the organization avatar used by the installed application launcher.

- Approved raster: `approved/home-health-hub.png`
- Current SVG classification: hybrid
- Shared palette: deep teal, hub teal, aqua, and pale aqua
- Prohibited additions: cloud imagery, manufacturer logos, arbitrary health readings, and medical claims

The approved raster and its hybrid SVG are authoritative and preserve the required hand shape and direction exactly. Do not reinterpret the hands or composition merely to produce a native vector.
